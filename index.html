About

Pilsen Photo is the content arm of FatCat Asset Management.

Founded and run by Dan Cruz, who holds a B.A. in Communication, Media, and Theatre — the same background now driving the on-camera coaching and creator development work above. Not a crew that just presses record, but one that knows how to direct a performance.

We started shooting our own properties, then our own products, then other people's everything. Based in Pilsen, we know the light, the walls, and the angles that make Chicago look like Chicago.

Our clients are streamers, YouTubers, vloggers, filmmakers, animators, and musicians who need content that performs — not just content that exists. If it doesn't grow your channel, we didn't do our job.

Part of the same team behind FatCat Cruz, NOD Academy, and Dan Cruz Homes.
